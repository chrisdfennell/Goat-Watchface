Description  (2,880 characters - the store allows 4,000)
========================================================
A goat looks back at you from your wrist, and it does not sit still.

Every second your watch redraws, the goat moves. It blinks. It chews its cud to
a slow rhythm. It flicks an ear at a fly and lets it settle. It glances off to
one side and its head follows a beat later. Now and then it shakes its head,
sticks out a pink tongue, or has a proper wide-jawed yawn with its eyes screwed
shut.

None of it is a canned loop. The pose is worked out from the clock itself, so
the goat is caught mid-fidget whenever you happen to look down.

SIXTEEN GOATS
Each breed has its own coat, markings, ears, horns and beard:

- Nubian - glossy chocolate, ears hanging past the jaw
- Nigerian Dwarf - chocolate and white, blue eyes, wattles, short kid face
- Alpine - black face with two white eye stripes, swept horns
- Boer - rust-red head, broad white blaze, heavy floppy ears
- Angora - cream fleece in ringlets, corkscrew horns, long beard
- Pygmy - agouti grey with a dark bandit mask
- LaMancha - the earless one, chocolate with tiny elf ears
- Toggenburg - fawn with two white stripes down the face
- Saanen - all white, pink muzzle, pale eyes
- Wild Ibex - huge ridged scimitar horns
- Black Bengal - jet black with amber eyes
- Oberhasli - deep bay red under a black mask
- Valais Blacknose - white ringlets, face black to the ears
- Markhor - wild corkscrew horns and a beard to the chest
- Golden Guernsey - blonde, ears carried low
- Kiko - hardy cream with a big sweep of horn

Pick your favourite, or leave it on Surprise Me for a new goat every hour, or
every day.

THE TIME
The time is stamped into the leather noseband of the goat's halter, in the
largest numeric font your watch can fit, and the date is written across its
forehead. Prefer bare digits? Turn the strap off.

SETTINGS
- Goat: surprise hourly, surprise daily, or any of the sixteen
- Liveliness: Still, Normal or Frisky - turn the fidgeting down, or off
- Backdrop: Auto (dawn, daylight, dusk, night), Meadow, Slate or Black
- Halter strap: on or off
- Time colour: cream, white, gold, mint or sky
- Show date: on or off
- Two labelled data fields: steps, heart rate, calories, battery, Body Battery,
  notifications, distance or active minutes

DRAWN, NOT PHOTOGRAPHED
There is no bitmap art in this watch face. Every horn, ear and rectangular pupil
is drawn by the watch itself, in shapes measured against your exact screen, so
it stays crisp on every panel from 218x218 up to 454x454 - and the colours are
chosen to hold their shape on 64-colour memory-in-pixel displays as well as on
AMOLED.

On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows a dim outline goat with its slot pupils
and the time, nudged a few pixels each minute to protect the panel.

Goats have rectangular pupils. They have wattles. They chew sideways. The
details are the whole point.
